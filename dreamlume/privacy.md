# Dreamlume Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 19, 2026

Dreamlume helps you record dreams, generate AI-based reflections, and manage your journal in a private and portable way. This page explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information you provide

When you use Dreamlume, you may enter dream titles, descriptions, tags, moods, and other journal details. This content is used only to power the app features you choose to use, including dream history, exports, and AI-generated reflections.

## How dream analysis works

If you request a dream analysis, the dream content you entered may be sent to the app's configured AI provider to generate the report shown in the app. This may include the text of your dream, tags, and selected mood, because those inputs are necessary to produce the analysis.

## Analytics and diagnostics

Dreamlume uses third-party services to understand app stability and product usage:

- Firebase Analytics for product analytics
- Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics
- Firebase Remote Config for runtime configuration
- RevenueCat for subscription status and purchase handling when subscriptions are enabled

These services may receive device or app usage metadata such as app version, crash data, and feature interaction events.

## Local storage and exports

Dream journal entries are stored locally on your device. If you export your journal or an analysis as PDF, the exported file is generated only when you request it and is then handled through the standard iOS share sheet.

## Your choices

- You can delete your dream entries from inside the app.
- You can clear all locally stored app data using the settings screen.
- You can export your journal before deleting it.

## Children's privacy

Dreamlume is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
